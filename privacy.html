Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 10, 2026

This application is a private, single-user integration operated solely for the Google account owner's personal use.

Data accessed

After the owner grants OAuth permission, the integration may access files and folders in the owner's Google Drive and may create, read, update or delete Google Drive files, Google Docs and Google Sheets only in response to the owner's instructions.

Use of data

Google user data is used only to perform actions explicitly requested by the owner. It is not used for advertising, profiling, sale, or unrelated purposes.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties. Data may be processed by the owner's private Hermes Agent server solely to carry out the owner's commands.

Storage and security

OAuth credentials are stored on the owner's private server and are not intentionally exposed publicly. Temporary files created while performing a requested operation are removed when they are no longer needed, subject to the owner's archival instructions.

Retention and deletion

The integration retains only the credentials and operational data needed to provide the requested connection. The owner can revoke access at any time from the Google Account security settings and may delete the stored authorization configuration from the private server.

Contact

Questions may be submitted through the GitHub repository issue tracker.
